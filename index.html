Timing depends on the job. Rush orders are available, so tell us your deadline on WhatsApp and we will confirm when your order will be ready.

How do I send my design files?

Send them on WhatsApp at +92 311 1151411 with the size and quantity you need. We check every file for free before printing.

By bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash, once you have approved your proof.
