What we print

Business cards

Flyers and brochures

A5, A4 and DL sizes. Folded or flat, single or double sided.

Banners and flex

Stickers and labels

How ordering works

No cart and no account. The whole order happens in one WhatsApp chat.

  1. 1

    Send your files

    Share your design or idea on WhatsApp with the size and quantity. No design? We can create one.

  2. 2

    Get a quote and proof

    We reply within working hours with the price, paper options and a digital proof.

  3. 3

    Approve and pay

    Confirm the proof and pay by bank transfer, Easypaisa or JazzCash. Printing starts right away.

  4. 4

    Pick up or get it delivered

    Collect from our office or get it couriered to your door anywhere in the world.
